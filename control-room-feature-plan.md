# EDGE | AGENCY Control Room — feature plan
Source: Manus "EDGE | AGENCY Control Room" web project, as shown in Kevin's screenshot 2026-11-12. Build PAUSED (Manus out of credits). This plan is the spec if/when Pip rebuilds it.

- CEO control-room overview: executive status, priority alerts, recommended actions
- C-suite health view: leadership functions, ownership, current status, trend
- Revenue and pipeline KPI reporting: targets, variance, forecasts, funnel stages
- Meta Ads performance view: spend, ROAS, CPA, conversion, creative and campaign breakdowns
- Approval queue: realistic items, filters, compact/detail states, evidence and risk shown per item
- Active experiments tracker: hypothesis, owner, stage, confidence, success criteria
- Client delivery health dashboard: account status, milestones, utilization/pacing, risks
- Daily operating flow: organizes the executive review into a clear sequence
- Interactive prototype controls: switch dashboard views, change date ranges
- Design system: premium EDGE | AGENCY, electric blue, generous spacing, high contrast
